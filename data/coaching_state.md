#### S001 — Framework and Standard Setting: Principles for Agentic Trust
- Situation: At Sling, non-deterministic AI behavior required an audit model beyond deterministic usability checks.

- Action: Developed Principles for Agentic Trust around Alignment, Execution, Control, and Calibration.
- Result: Accepted at ACM CSCW 2026 Industry Perspectives.
- Earned Secret: Trust in agentic systems is not a global attitude; it must be calibrated separately to whether the system intends the right goal, executes reliably, remains controllable, and communicates uncertainty well enough for the human to intervene.
