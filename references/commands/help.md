# help — Focused Command Guide

Read `coaching_state.md`, then show only commands relevant to the Anduril Air Defense interview.

## Recommendation Logic

- Unverified company, team, interviewer, or keynote claim -> `research`.
- Unresolved Lead/Staff objections -> `concerns`.
- Weak or unvalidated story evidence -> `stories improve [S###]`.
- Lowest readiness area is Research Thesis or Empirical Rigor -> `practice kim`.
- Lowest area is Systems Integration or Operational Judgment -> `practice systems`.
- Lowest area is Research Velocity or roadmap influence -> `practice product`.
- Lowest area is interaction architecture or ergonomics -> `practice design`.
- Interview within 48 hours -> `prep`, then one highest-risk persona drill.
- Transcript available -> `analyze`.
- Three or more scored sessions -> `progress`.
