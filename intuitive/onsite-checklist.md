## Onsite logistics

- ☐ Book Sunnyvale and Irvine as one trip. Arrive the night before, and allow a 30-minute buffer.
- ☐ If there's a presentation, rehearse it to 18 minutes for a 20-minute slot. Bring a PDF backup on a USB drive and in your email.
- ☐ Bring printed copies of the updated résumé and a notebook. Write down each interviewer's name and one thing they said, for the thank-you notes.
- ☐ Send thank-you notes within 24 hours: one line of personal reference each, plus one short sentence following through on anything you'd want to add.
- ☐ Email Reema the same day. Restate your interest, name one thing that sharpened your thinking, and ask about timeline so you can line it up with your other process.
